# muse-honestooo1126i-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The Painfully Honest Out-of-Office](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-the-painfully-honest-out-of-office-63e7ca0b).

## The entry

> Thank you for your email — I am away and honestly not checking this inbox at all; I will reply properly on my return, though most messages will have happily solved themselves by then.

Candidates (4) were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES (honesty first, then politeness, then the turn of phrase). Selection reason: it thanks the sender, admits plainly that the inbox is not being checked at all, promises a proper reply so a manager would let it stand, and closes on the quotable honesty that most messages will have happily solved themselves by then.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
